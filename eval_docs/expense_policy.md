# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Meal Expenses

Meal reimbursement requires an itemized receipt. The daily meal limit is 60 USD for domestic travel and 90 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved for a client event.

## Lodging

Hotel reimbursement is capped at 220 USD per night for domestic travel and 300 USD per night for international travel. Exceptions require director approval before booking.

## Transportation

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more with manager approval. Business class requires VP approval regardless of flight duration.

## Submission Deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Reports submitted after 30 days require finance manager approval. Reimbursements are usually paid within 7 business days after approval.

## Missing Receipts

If a receipt is missing, employees must submit a missing receipt declaration. Declarations are limited to 3 uses per quarter. Expenses above 100 USD still require finance approval when the original receipt is unavailable.
