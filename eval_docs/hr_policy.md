# HR Policy Handbook

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 15 paid annual leave days per calendar year. New employees who join after July 1 receive 7 annual leave days for that first calendar year. Unused annual leave can carry over to the next year, but the carryover limit is 5 days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive workdays. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave balance.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote work from another country requires approval from HR and Security at least 10 business days before travel.

## Probation

New employees have a 3-month probation period. The manager must complete a probation review before the end of the third month. Probation can be extended once for up to 2 additional months.

## Employment Verification

Employees can request employment verification letters from HR Operations. Standard letters are issued within 3 business days. Letters that include salary details require written employee consent.
